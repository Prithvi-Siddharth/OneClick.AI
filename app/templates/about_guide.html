OneClick.AI User Guide

Welcome to OneClick.AI! This guide will help you navigate our platform and build your first machine learning model.

1. Registration & Login

To get started, you need an account. Click on the Register button on the home page. Provide a unique username, email, and password. Once registered, log in to access your personal dashboard.

2. The Dashboard

Your dashboard is the central hub. Here you can see an overview of your recent activity, including uploaded datasets and trained models. Use the quick action buttons to jump straight into tasks.

3. Uploading Data

Before you can train a model, you need data.

  • Go to the Data Catalog or click "Upload Dataset" on the dashboard.
  • Upload your CSV file. Ensure it has headers (column names).
  • Provide a name and description for your dataset.

4. Preprocessing

Raw data often needs cleaning.

  • Navigate to the Preprocess tab in the dashboard.
  • Use our tools to handle missing values, encode categorical variables, and normalize numerical features.
  • Save your processed dataset for training.

5. Training a Model

This is where the magic happens.

  • Go to the Model Training tab in the dashboard.
  • Select your preprocessed dataset.
  • Choose the target column (what you want to predict).
  • Select an algorithm (e.g., Logistic Regression, Random Forest).
  • Configure hyperparameters or use default settings.
  • Click "Train" and wait for the results!

6. Evaluating & Using Models

Once trained, you can view your model's performance metrics (Accuracy, F1-Score, etc.).

  • Download the trained model artifact for offline use.
  • Use the platform to make predictions on new data (coming soon).
